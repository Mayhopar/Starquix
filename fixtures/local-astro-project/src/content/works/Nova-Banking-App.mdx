Nova started as a compliance-driven rebuild of a legacy retail bank and turned
into a full product reset. We threw away the account-first navigation and
organized the whole app around money movement: pay, save, and see where it went.

## The problem

The old app had **41 screens** to make a single transfer. Support tickets told
the same story every week:

- People couldn't find their own IBAN.
- Recurring payments were buried three menus deep.
- The savings product had a 9% activation rate despite a 4.5% APY.

<DoubleImage src1="./images/timepad/grid-1.png" src2="./images/timepad/grid-2.png" caption="Left: the legacy transfer flow. Right: the same job in Nova — two taps." />

## What we changed

Every screen answers one question: *what can I do with this money right now?*
The home feed mixes balances with actions, so the most common jobs — send,
split, top up — are never more than one tap from launch.

> "It's the first banking app where I don't feel like I'm filing taxes."
> — beta tester, week 3

<SingleImage src="/uploads/55445954468_822ed2e16a_k.jpg" alt="Design system audit wall" />

## Results

1. Transfer completion time dropped from 74s to 11s.
2. Savings activation went from 9% to 38% in the first quarter.
3. App-store rating climbed from 2.9 to 4.7.
